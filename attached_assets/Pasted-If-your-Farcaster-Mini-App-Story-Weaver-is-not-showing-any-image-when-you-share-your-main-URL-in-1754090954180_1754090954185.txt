If your Farcaster Mini App ("Story Weaver") is not showing any image when you share your main URL in a cast and expect to see a preview (embed) with your image and button, the most common reason is that **embed meta tags are missing from your web page**. Here’s what you need to do to fix it:

### Why Your Image Isn’t Showing

- The `farcaster.json` manifest registers your app and its actions for discovery and usage.
- **But for casts to show a preview image (embed) and a button, each shareable URL (like your home page) must also include a special `` tag in its HTML ``, describing the image, button, and action.** This is similar to how OpenGraph meta tags work for social previews, but using Farcaster’s format[1][2][3][4].

   - Clear your browser/Farcaster cache or use their “Refetch”/Preview Embed tool[3][5].
   - Ensure the image is publicly accessible, loads via direct link, and returns HTTP 200 with `image/*` Content-Type.

### Reference/Why This Works

- **Manifest** (`farcaster.json`) = discovery & enable actions in clients.
- **Embed meta tag** (`fc:miniapp`) = enables rich image+button previews for the exact URL you share in casts.
- **Both are required for optimal Farcaster Mini App integration**[1][2][4].
